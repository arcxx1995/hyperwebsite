# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General public — motivated individuals who want to learn to build real things with AI but don't know where to start. They arrive skeptical of hype, aware that most "learn AI" content is shallow. They're on the fence: curious enough to browse, cautious enough to not sign up easily. They need to feel that Hyperspark is the real thing.

## Product Purpose

Hyperspark is a Building with AI school. The site's job is to convert visitors into enrolled students. Success means someone who arrived uncertain leaves with their payment submitted or spot reserved.

## Brand Personality

Bold, urgent, energetic — but through precision and confidence, not volume or noise. The urgency is earned: a clear articulation of what's possible and why now. Closer to a compelling coach than a hype machine.

## Anti-references

- Generic bootcamp sites: countdown timers, fake scarcity, hustle-culture copy, bright yellow CTAs
- Over-designed / AI-slop aesthetic: glassmorphism, neon glow, gradient text, visual noise for its own sake
- Academic / institutional: walls of text, dry course catalogs, no personality

## Design Principles

1. **Precision as energy** — Urgency through sharpness and specificity, not shouting. A tight grid and deliberate type scale communicate discipline.
2. **Earn every word** — No buzzwords, no padding. Each sentence should say something a competitor's site couldn't.
3. **Dark-mode native** — Linear/Vercel-grade craft: high contrast, minimal chrome, surfaces that feel authoritative.
4. **Motion with intent** — Energetic doesn't mean chaotic. Animation should guide attention, not distract from substance.
5. **Remove the hesitation** — Every section answers an unspoken objection. The site removes barriers; it doesn't just list features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support required for all animations. Copy and flow must work without prior AI literacy — assume curious beginner, not developer.
